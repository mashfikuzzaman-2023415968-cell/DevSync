## Role assessments (newcomers)

Until you've finished three projects on DevSync, you bid as a newcomer. For each role you want
to bid on, you take a short **role assessment** built for that kind of role: everyone bidding
for it takes the same one.

1. Open the project and choose the role. The card shows what the assessment covers and why.
2. Choose **Start assessment**, read the rules and accept them. It opens in full screen.
3. Answer one question at a time; you can't go back. Leaving full screen or the tab counts as
   leaving: the first time is a warning, the second ends the assessment.
4. You'll see your score per competency. With a pass (60 overall, no main competency under 40)
   you can place your bid; the pass counts for roles like it for 180 days. Otherwise you can
   retake it after 30 days. Your bid draft is kept while you take the assessment.

**Practice tests** (under Practice tests) help you prepare and show on your profile as
practice results; they don't count for bidding unless a role has no assessment yet.
